Night Owl Cafés

Amber ring = about 45 minutes on foot. Tap a pin to jump to that cafe.
From: Downtown Berkeley BART

      About this list

      Only cafes that stay open past 3 PM are included. Hours, prices and notes were gathered in early November 2026 from Yelp, Google, Apple Maps, delivery listings, UC Berkeley student guides and local news. Hours change often, so check before a late walk.

      Study and bathroom scores are 1–5 summaries of what reviewers say (seating, Wi-Fi, outlets, noise, restroom access). Bathroom scores marked “unconfirmed” had no specific reviewer comments and are set to a neutral 3.

      Walking time is estimated from straight-line distance plus 30% for street layout, at about 3 mph. Chai Thai Tea is the Thai tea shop at 1796 Shattuck, and Derda is Dera Cafe at 1620 Shattuck.
